Frequently Asked Questions

You can pay your water bill directly at the Janopol Oriental Waterworks cashier office, Monday to Friday, from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
A 10% penalty charge is automatically applied to unpaid accounts after the due date. Continued non-payment may lead to temporary disconnection.
Please present a valid Senior Citizen ID and proof of residence at our office to register your account for the mandatory senior discount rate.

43rd Anniversary of Darasa Rural Waterworks

Marking an incredible legacy of community service, Darasa Rural Waterworks has proudly provided dependable and continuous water supply to the locality for forty-three years since its establishment. Over more than four decades of operation, the waterworks has remained a cornerstone of local development.
