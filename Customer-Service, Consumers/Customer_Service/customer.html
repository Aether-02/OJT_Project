Frequently Asked Questions
You can pay your water bill online through our Billing & Payments section. We accept GCash, Maya, and bank transfers. Simply log in, view your statement, and click "Pay Now" to proceed with your payment.
New connection applications typically take 5–10 business days to process, depending on the area and requirements. You can track your application status online through our portal.
Discolored water can be caused by pipe maintenance or sediments. Run your tap for a few minutes until the water clears. If the problem persists, report it through our Report Leak or Interruption section and our team will respond promptly.
You can update your account information by visiting our office with a valid ID, or through our online portal. For name changes or address updates, please bring supporting documents.
Our office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. For emergencies, our 24/7 hotline is available at 043-XXX-XXXX.
